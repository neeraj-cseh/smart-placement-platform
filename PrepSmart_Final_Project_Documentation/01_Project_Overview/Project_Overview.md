## 1. Project Overview

### Purpose
The Smart Placement Platform is an integrated, full-stack educational web application designed to streamline and structure the placement preparation process for college students. It provides a single ecosystem for curriculum learning, coding practice, mock assessments, and AI-driven interview preparation.

### Vision
To democratize high-quality placement preparation by bridging the gap between academic curricula and industry expectations through a unified, data-driven, and highly interactive digital platform.

### Motivation
Placement preparation is notoriously fragmented. Students typically rely on multiple disjointed platforms—one for learning concepts, another for coding practice, and yet another for mock tests or interview prep. This fragmentation leads to a lack of coherent progress tracking and structured learning. The motivation was to build a centralized, "smart" platform that unifies these ecosystems.

### Real-World Problem
- **Unstructured Preparation**: Students lack guided roadmaps tailored to specific domains (DSA, Core CS, Aptitude).
- **Feedback Vacuum**: Practicing interviews in isolation provides no constructive feedback.
- **Siloed Analytics**: Without a unified platform, students cannot identify their true weaknesses across different competencies.
